Good design is felt before it is explained.

Zaksy Studio is a premier interior and furniture design firm in Hyderabad founded by Zakwan Syed. We specialize in creating bespoke residential and commercial spaces that blend modern aesthetics with everyday functionality.

Operating from Saidabad and serving discerning clients across Hyderabad, Hitec City, and the Financial District, every commission begins with an inquiry into human emotion: How should you feel when you wake up in your master bedroom? What sense of quiet sanctuary should welcome you in your private bath? What atmosphere sparks conversation in a dining room?
