04 / METHODOLOGY THE 4-STAGE ARCHITECTURAL JOURNEY

How It Works: Precision Process.

A transparent architectural roadmap designed to eliminate uncertainty and deliver uncompromised quality on schedule.

PHASE 01 • WEEKS 1–2

Spatial Diagnosis & Vision

We begin by listening. Understanding your daily habits, morning routines, sun paths, acoustic challenges, and functional zoning priorities.

DELIVERABLE: Spatial Zoning Schematics & Material Mood Direction
PHASE 02 • WEEKS 3–5

Materiality & CAD Schematics

Detailed technical drawings: reflected ceiling plans, lighting Kelvin schedules, plumbing drops, 3D photorealistic views, and stone vein selections.

DELIVERABLE: Complete Architectural Execution Drawings & BOQ
PHASE 03 • WEEKS 6–10

Artisan Joinery & Procurement

Handcrafting custom millwork in our atelier, selecting stone blocks directly from quarry yards in Hyderabad, and testing brass metalwork patinas.

DELIVERABLE: Physical Material Sample Sign-Off & Joinery Mockups
PHASE 04 • WEEKS 11–16

Turnkey Execution & Handover

Dedicated site engineers ensure zero tolerance deviation. Final hardware calibration, architectural deep clean, art curation, and white-glove key handover.

DELIVERABLE: Completed Sanctuary Ready for Pure Living
