01 Studio Scope & Entity

This Privacy Policy and Terms of Service govern the relationship between ZAKSY STUDIO ® ("Studio", "we", "us", or "our"), an independent interior architecture and spatial design practice operating in Saidabad, Hyderabad, Telangana, India, and individuals or entities ("Client", "User", or "you") accessing our digital website or engaging our architectural design services.

By visiting this website or contacting us via our telephone/WhatsApp channels (+91 91007 86726), you acknowledge and agree to the data collection and operational practices described herein.

02 Data We Collect

We strictly limit data collection to details essential for architectural design consultations and execution. Information collected includes:

  • Contact Information: Full name, telephone number, WhatsApp contact handle, and email address.
  • Property & Spatial Information: Site address, floor plans, CAD drawings, ceiling heights, civil contractor notes, structural layout files, and photographs of the property.
  • Project Preferences: Functional requirements, aesthetic references, lifestyle preferences, and targeted budget parameters.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized browser type, device resolution, and page response times to optimize the 300-frame visual canvas rendering performance.

03 How Information is Used

Your spatial and personal information is utilized exclusively for:

  • Preparing tailored spatial schematics, bedroom layouts, travertine bathroom plans, and bespoke millwork designs.
  • Coordinating on-site inspections, surveys, and architectural reviews in Hyderabad and associated project locations.
  • Procurement coordination with vetted stone masons, joinery artisans, lighting vendors, and civil contractors.
  • Delivering invoices, milestone progress reports, and design phase documentations.

Zero Data Monetization: Zaksy Studio never sells, rents, leases, or trades your personal or project data to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing syndicates.

Upon receipt of contracted design fees, the Client receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to execute the design specifically at the designated site location. Designs may not be re-sold, mass-reproduced, or transferred to other properties without prior written consent from Zaksy Studio.

06 Client Confidentiality & Project Photography

We deeply respect the sanctuary of private homes:

  • Residential Privacy: Exact residential addresses, security configurations, and identifying personal details are strictly confidential.
  • Portfolio Photography: Zaksy Studio reserves the right to capture architectural portfolio photography of completed spaces. However, no personal client possessions, family portraits, or sensitive personal elements are ever included, and client identities are kept anonymous in public showcases unless explicitly permitted.

07 Terms of Engagement & Turnkey Execution

All architectural and interior design engagements proceed through defined contractual stages:

  • Stage I — Concept & Spatial Strategy: Floor plan zoning, circulation studies, and material palette direction.
  • Stage II — Detailed Architectural Drawings: Plumbing layouts, reflected ceiling plans (RCP), joinery sections, and electrical lighting schedules.
  • Stage III — Procurement & Turnkey Execution: Site coordination, material quality checks, and final handover.

Written sign-off is required at each milestone before proceeding to subsequent procurement or civil construction phases.
